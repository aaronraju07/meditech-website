TG-880 C4 IN Incubator Ambulance Stretcher

SKU: MED-STR-TG880-C4IN

The TG-880 C4 IN is KARTSANA's dedicated incubator-transport ambulance stretcher — purpose-engineered to carry neonatal and paediatric incubators securely during emergency medical transit. Built from high-grade structural aluminium, the C4 IN delivers a proven 280 kg unassisted load capacity, four-wheel independent locking stability, and a certified 10G crash-safe chassis. Its slim 585 mm width and low-profile 986 mm standing height make it the ideal solution for tight ambulance bays, and it ships with confirmed compatibility with multiple Jupiter Compact rail systems including R-419L, R-303L, and R-499N.

  • ✓ Purpose-Built Incubator Platform: Frame geometry and mounting deck engineered specifically to interface with neonatal and paediatric incubators for critical mobile transport.
  • ✓ 280 kg Unassisted Load Capacity: Heavy-duty aluminum chassis delivers full unassisted structural support up to 280 kg — incubator and patient combined load.
  • ✓ Multi-Rail System Compatibility: Confirmed factory compatibility with R-419L, R-303L, and R-499N Jupiter Compact ambulance floor rail systems for maximum fleet flexibility.

Incubator Transport Engineering & Rail Compatibility

The TG-880 C4 IN represents the specialist evolution of KARTSANA's proven TG-880 stretcher platform, redesigned from the frame up to accommodate the unique weight distribution and mounting requirements of clinical neonatal incubators. The high-grade aluminium chassis maintains the exceptional rigidity and crash-safe structural properties of the standard C4 series while introducing an optimised central platform deck width of 585 mm — tight enough to navigate ambulance loading channels without compromising incubator stability.


Mobility is delivered through four independently lockable wheel assemblies, enabling EMS crews to engage full-chassis braking on any surface at any point in the loading or transfer workflow. The TG-880 C4 IN's rail compatibility has been factory-tested and confirmed across multiple Jupiter Compact floor rail configurations: the R-419L (standard rail), R-303L (compact rail), and R-499N (narrow rail) — providing complete flexibility across mixed ambulance fleet environments. All KARTSANA products, including the C4 IN, carry full compliance certification under EN 1865 and EN 1789 European ambulance equipment standards and are independently validated to the 10G dynamic crash safety threshold.

Jupiter Compact Series · Ambulance Floor Rail System

R-419 / R-419L

Range of Rails for JUPITER COMPACT Ambulance Stretchers — Confirmed Compatible with TG-880 C4 IN

The R-419 / R-419L is a factory-certified ambulance floor rail system designed for the JUPITER COMPACT stretcher series and confirmed compatible with the TG-880 C4 IN. Built from combined aluminum and steel construction, it provides a rigid, secure track for incubator stretcher transit. The system features a manual locking/unlocking entry ramp for controlled stretcher docking, an integrated backboard stretcher drawer for dedicated emergency equipment storage, and full structural certification to the 10G dynamic crash standard — matching the crash safety level of the stretcher itself.

  • ✓ Factory-Confirmed TG-880 C4 IN Compatibility: The R-419L is verified by the manufacturer for direct use with the TG-880 C4 IN, eliminating guesswork and ensuring first-time fitment in JUPITER COMPACT-configured ambulances.
  • ✓ Backboard & Scoop Stretcher Storage: Built-in drawer provides secure in-ambulance storage for a full-size backboard or scoop stretcher, keeping critical rescue equipment instantly accessible without occupying additional cabin space.
  • ✓ Manual Ramp Lock / Unlock: Precise manual locking ramp mechanism positively engages and releases the stretcher from the rail without tools, enabling fast, controlled loading and unloading for incubator transport workflows.
  • ✓ 10G Dynamic Crash Certified: Every structural element of the R-419 rail system is independently validated under the 10G crash standard — the same benchmark as the TG-880 C4 IN — ensuring the complete stretcher-rail assembly meets a unified safety certification.

Compatibility Note: The R-419 / R-419L rail system is factory-confirmed compatible with the TG-880 C4 IN. The R-419 suits standard ambulance bays; the R-419L is recommended for extended-wheelbase or high-specification incubator transport vehicles. Contact our team to confirm the optimal rail variant for your fleet.

Why the TG-880 C4 IN + R-419 Rail Is the Complete Incubator Transport Solution

Transporting a neonatal incubator by ambulance demands a level of structural certainty beyond standard patient transport. The TG-880 C4 IN provides the incubator-optimised platform; the R-419 / R-419L provides the certified floor anchorage. Together, they form a dual 10G-certified chain from incubator deck to ambulance floor — guaranteeing that every component the patient depends on is independently validated to the same crash safety standard. The integrated backboard drawer further ensures that resuscitation-critical equipment travels in the same secured envelope, eliminating unsecured loose equipment in the patient compartment.
