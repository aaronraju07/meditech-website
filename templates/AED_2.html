MCL Saver One P Defibrillator

SKU: MED-DEFIB-SAVERONEP

The Saver One P is a tough, small, and lightweight professional defibrillator that provides unmatched flexibility for any rescue scenario. Operating as a standard AED by default for BLS rescuers, it can be instantly switched into a Manual Defibrillator to give ALS responders complete decision-making control over manual shock timing or synchronized electric cardioversion. Equipped with an integrated 5.7" TFT color display and a widely manageable software program, it delivers total device customization to suit professional emergency medical workflows.

  • ✓ Triple Mode Versatility: Seamlessly serves as an automated external defibrillator (AED), a manual override defibrillator, or a basic cardiac monitoring device.
  • ✓ High-Resolution 5.7" TFT Display: Provides live data tracking including real-time ECG waveforms, heart rate, battery gauges, memory indicators, and rescue timers.
  • ✓ Advanced PBLS Feature: Allows healthcare providers to switch to a strict 15:2 compression-to-ventilation ratio during pediatric basic life support protocols with multiple rescuers.

Clinical Overview & Monitoring Capabilities

Engineered for high-stress advanced life support interventions, the MCL Saver One P utilizes an adaptive Biphase Truncated Exponential (BTE) discharge waveform that conforms automatically to the patient's chest impedance profile. For electric cardioversion therapies executed in synchronous manual override mode, the device intelligently synchronizes the energy discharge to match the exact point of the R-wave on the monitored ECG.


Its high-definition display screen functions as a comprehensive diagnostic window, showing crucial text alarms and audible alerts alongside live metric charts. The system continuously outputs a touch/not touch pictogram, an active CPR bar graph with a cycle countdown, precise impedance measurements, total shock counts, and delivered energy settings. The integrated ECG monitoring can be managed through standard defibrillation pads or via standalone ECG electrodes hooked up to an independent, reusable 2-Lead patient monitoring cable (Code SAV-C0017).
