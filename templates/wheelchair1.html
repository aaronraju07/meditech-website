Mechanical Evacuation Chairs Series (S-127 / S-129 / S-129 RG)

SKU: MED-ESC-S12X-SERIES

The Mechanical Evacuation Chair Series delivers exceptional versatility and portable reliability for high-stress emergency stair descent and tactical patient egress applications. Constructed from top-tier, ultra-lightweight structural aluminium, these highly durable folding chairs feature resilient designs engineered to sustain a reliable load capacity of up to 150 kg. Outfitted with professional multi-point safety belt restraint kits, they guarantee maximum patient security and steady tracking during rapid transport operations.

  • ✓ Aluminium Folding Architecture: Built using ultra-handy, resilient aluminium frame layouts that fold down exceptionally tight for compact vehicular storage.
  • ✓ Advanced Handling Configurations: The S-129 and S-129 RG systems feature an integrated arrangement of 6 independent maneuvering handles (4 collapsible + 2 extendable) for optimized stair tracking.
  • ✓ Multi-Point Security Restraints: Outfitted with up to 3 specialized heavy-duty safety belts and integrated folding footrests to properly secure the body under transit.

Clinical Ergonomics & Model Configurations

Designed to satisfy modern institutional safety thresholds during critical building egress or emergency mobile transports, this KARTSANA mechanical chair series minimizes structural friction across various transit settings. Each model option is specifically optimized around localized emergency maneuvering challenges:


Model S-127: A highly compact configuration featuring a sleek aluminium folding frame enhanced with a premium protective epoxy paint finish. It comes equipped with 2 directional wheels and a 2-point safety belt restraint kit, making it an excellent lightweight choice for straightforward single-level or ramp transfers.


Model S-129: Built for multi-level stair environments, this variant integrates an expanded 4-wheel tracking system and a comprehensive 3-point safety belt restraint kit. The frame features a durable epoxy paint finish, an integrated folding footrest, and an advanced 6-handle steering cluster (4 collapsible handles for tight turns and 2 extendable handles for stable stair descent control).


Model S-129 RG: The premier heavy-duty configuration within the mechanical series. It incorporates all the advanced 6-handle steering clusters, 4-wheel geometry, integrated folding footrests, and 3-point security belt parameters of the S-129, but utilizes a specialized, bare structural high-capacity aluminium layout to maximize frame rigidity for rugged, long-term deployment.
