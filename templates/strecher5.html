Autoloader Collapsible Stretcher (Ambulance Stretcher)

SKU: MED-STR-MAMSTAS101  |  Model: MAM STAS 101

The MAM STAS 101 is a CE certified automatic loading stretcher engineered from strong, corrosion-resistant aluminium alloy. Designed for fast, safe, and efficient patient transfer in emergency vehicles, it features automatic folding legs that simplify ambulance loading, a foamed cushion for patient comfort, and an adjustable backrest that reclines up to 75° for varying transport positions. Dual-end control handles and a secure ambulance locking system make it a dependable choice for hospitals, ambulance fleets, and emergency response teams.

  • ✓ Automatic Folding Legs: Legs fold automatically during ambulance loading, removing the need for manual leg collapse and speeding up patient transfer in time-critical situations.
  • ✓ 159 kg Load Bearing Capacity: Strong, corrosion-resistant aluminium alloy frame rated to safely support a wide range of patient body types.
  • ✓ Adjustable Backrest to 75°: Reclining backrest accommodates seated, semi-reclined, or fully flat transport positions to suit patient condition.
  • ✓ Secure Locking & Dual-End Handles: Ambulance locking system keeps the stretcher securely fixed in transit, while control handles at both ends give crew confident handling during loading and unloading.

Fast, Safe Patient Transfer for Emergency Vehicles

The MAM STAS 101 Autoloader Collapsible Stretcher is purpose-built for the demands of modern ambulance operations, where every second of loading and unloading time matters. Its automatic folding leg mechanism engages as the stretcher is loaded into the vehicle, removing a manual step that can slow crews during high-pressure transfers, and reducing the physical strain on operators handling repeated loading cycles across a shift.


Constructed from strong, corrosion-resistant aluminium alloy, the frame balances structural rigidity with manageable weight at just 40 kg net. The foamed cushion surface improves patient comfort during transport, while the backrest's adjustable range up to 75° allows crews to position patients according to their clinical condition — from fully reclined to a more upright seated angle for respiratory or cardiac cases.


Dual-end control handles give responders confident grip and maneuvering control during loading, unloading, and ward transfers, and the integrated ambulance locking system secures the stretcher firmly in place during transit. Manufactured in India by Deluxe Scientific Surgico Pvt. Ltd., a company with decades of medical equipment manufacturing heritage, the MAM STAS 101 carries CE certification and is supplied to hospitals, ambulance services, sports facilities, and disaster response teams at wholesale competitive prices.
