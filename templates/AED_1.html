MCL Smarty Saver AED

SKU: MED-DEFIB-SMARTY

The Smarty Saver Series represents AMI Italia's latest portable defibrillator line, specifically engineered for quick and simple treatment of Sudden Cardiac Arrest (SCA) and to assist in delivering vital Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation (CPR). It is designed to be reliable, simple, and easy to use by anyone, whether they are medical professional responders or untrained bystanders. With its lightweight portability, integrated folding handle, and catching appearance, it meets the gold standard for early defibrillation in public large-scale application areas.

  • ✓ Instant Patient Mode Switch: Effortlessly toggle between Adult and Child modes using a single set of universal preconnected electrodes.
  • ✓ Superior Environmental Sealing: Certified IP56 dustproof and waterproof resistance, built to survive severe environments and a 1-meter drop test.
  • ✓ Ultra-Fast Administration: Fast shock countdown ready to discharge within 9 seconds at 150J or within 12 seconds at 200J.

Clinical Overview & Advanced Functionality

Conceived for rapid-response deployment across any environment, the Smarty Saver Series utilizes a high-performance, thorax impedance-compensated Biphase Truncated Exponential (BTE) discharge waveform. This technology automatically scales and adapts energy output parameters dynamically according to the patient's biological impedance profile. The advanced onboard software delivers voice prompts, clear audio-visual signals, and an integrated metronome tracking feature to guide operators seamlessly through the exact cycles of chest compressions and insufflations.


Semi-Automatic Model: Actively analyzes the patient's ECG profile. If a shockable cardiac rhythm is detected, it automatically charges its reservoir capacitor and directs the user to manually depress the physical shock button when prompted by vocal instructions.


Fully Automatic Model: If a shockable rhythm is flagged, this system issues a verbal alert warning of an imminent shock delivery, initiates a clear 5-second safety countdown, and automatically releases the therapeutic defibrillation discharge without requiring physical human button interaction.


Premium Smarty Series Ecosystem Extensions:
• SMARTY SaverPlus: Integrates dedicated premium modules for real-time quality feedback tracking during manual CPR chest compression intervals.
• SMARTY SaverGeo: Combines the real-time clinical CPR quality metrics evaluation with instant access synchronization parameters targeting the Amisavercloud management platform.
