MCL Saver One D Defibrillator

SKU: MED-DEFIB-SAVERONED

The Saver One D goes beyond standard emergency care protocols, providing a rugged, small, and lightweight semi-automatic AED with advanced ECG monitoring capabilities. Totally reliable for paramedics, medical emergency workers, and trained responders, it stands out as the ultimate choice for harsh weather conditions, outdoor fields, or mobile transport settings. Featuring a large (12 × 8 cm) full-color interactive screen, it guarantees that critical rescue metrics remain completely scannable at a glance.

  • ✓ Interactive 5.7" TFT Display: Delivers the most detailed and comprehensive interface screen for maximum situational awareness.
  • ✓ Dual Monitoring Layouts: Tracks patient rhythm and heart rates using standard defibrillation pads or via standalone ECG electrodes.
  • ✓ Adaptive Energy Escalation: Employs an adaptive BTE waveform adjusting dynamically to physical chest impedance with max settings up to 360J.

Clinical Overview & Device Ergonomics

Designed for professional responders operating in high-demand field scenarios, the MCL Saver One D provides unmatched structural and functional support. While running in default AED mode, the comprehensive color interface screen outputs an extensive range of system variables. The display window yields immediate data regarding device condition (including a residual capacity battery gauge, an indicator of available recording memory, active microphone status, and local date/time settings) alongside crucial active rescue metrics. Responders have immediate clarity on adult or child protocols, shock counts, elapsed rescue timers, real-time heart rate (bpm), physical thoracic impedance (ohms), upcoming delivery energy levels (joules), precise touch/not touch pictograms, and active CPR countdown bar graphs.


When tactical circumstances require extended monitoring without shock delivery, the unit seamlessly transitions to a dedicated ECG Monitoring mode. This system architecture ensures a reliable workflow while keeping the device small, highly portable, and deeply efficient under extreme parameters. Data tracking can be reviewed following the rescue event through the "Saver View Express" data manager software platform via USB or flash storage card pathways.
