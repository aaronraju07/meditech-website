TG-880 C4 / TG-880 C2 Ambulance Stretcher

SKU: MED-STR-TG880-SERIES

The TG-880 Series represents KARTSANA's premier line of high-strength, lightweight aluminum ambulance stretchers engineered to optimize emergency transit and patient transfer workflows. Combining robust unassisted structural load capacities up to 280 kg with advanced ergonomic reclinable frameworks, these stretchers deliver complete versatility for high-demand emergency medical service environments. With strict adherence to premium safety benchmarks, the TG-880 series guarantees structural reliance when tracking through the most demanding mobile rescue maneuvers.

  • ✓ Unassisted 280 kg Load Capacity: Premium architectural frame layout capable of lifting and sustaining up to a 280 kg patient load completely unassisted.
  • ✓ Ergonomic Multi-Position Framing: Engineered with 8 independent intermediary position adjustments paired with a reclinable layout for customized patient orientation.
  • ✓ Advanced Safety & Transfer Controls: Features laterally and longitudinally collapsible guardrails for seamless patient transfers, a double-jointed leg rest assembly, and full Trendelenburg / reverse Trendelenburg positioning capabilities.

Clinical Ergonomics & Safety Compliance

Designed to fulfill the rigorous demands of professional medical transit teams, the TG-880 C4 / TG-880 C2 Stretcher line provides unparalleled structural balance and ease of handling. Built completely from specialized high-grade aluminum alloys, the chassis ensures extreme physical rigidness while keeping total ambulance loading weight tightly controlled. The integration of collapsible guardrails that fold down both laterally and longitudinally simplifies critical patient transfers in tight roadside or institutional quarters.


Maneuverability and tracking are optimized through a heavy-duty wheel assembly configuration comprising 2 high-grade rotating wheels and 4 robust lockable wheels to ensure smooth transport stability across variable indoor and outdoor surfaces. All products within this premium KARTSANA manufacturing series come fully backed by official EN 1865 and EN 1789 certificates, meeting the strict 10G dynamic crash testing safety thresholds required for active integration into modern emergency vehicle configurations.

Jupiter Compact Series · Ambulance Floor Rail System

R-419 / R-419L

Range of Rails for JUPITER COMPACT Ambulance Stretchers

The R-419 / R-419L is a precision-engineered ambulance floor rail system purpose-built for the JUPITER COMPACT stretcher series. Constructed from a robust combination of aluminum and steel, it provides a rigid, secure anchoring track for the TG-880 C4/C2 during vehicle transit. The system integrates a manual locking/unlocking entry ramp mechanism for controlled stretcher loading, an integrated backboard stretcher drawer for quick-access emergency equipment storage, and full compatibility with backboard or scoop stretcher configurations — making it a comprehensive in-vehicle patient and equipment management platform.

  • ✓ Backboard & Scoop Stretcher Storage: Built-in drawer system provides secure in-ambulance storage for a full-size backboard or scoop stretcher, keeping critical rescue equipment instantly accessible without occupying additional cabin space.
  • ✓ Manual Ramp Lock / Unlock: Precision manual locking mechanism controls the integrated entry ramp, ensuring the stretcher is positively engaged with the rail during transit and can be swiftly released by crew without tools.
  • ✓ Aluminum & Steel Dual-Material Frame: The combination of lightweight aluminum body panels with reinforced steel locking hardware delivers maximum structural durability while keeping the rail system's installed weight fully within vehicle payload limits.
  • ✓ 10G Dynamic Crash Certified: Independently validated under the 10G dynamic crash standard, the R-419 rail system guarantees the complete restraint system — stretcher, rail, and drawer — maintains full structural integrity under extreme ambulance emergency braking forces.

Compatibility Note: The R-419 / R-419L rail system is designed for JUPITER COMPACT series ambulances and is fully compatible with the TG-880 C4 / TG-880 C2 stretcher lineup. Choose R-419 for standard ambulance bays or R-419L for extended-wheelbase vehicle configurations. Contact our team for a vehicle fitment assessment.

Why a Certified Ambulance Floor Rail Lock System Is Essential

An ambulance floor rail system is the critical link between the stretcher and the vehicle — its integrity directly determines patient safety during transit. Without a certified rail lock, even the most advanced stretcher becomes an unsecured hazard under emergency braking or collision forces. The R-419 / R-419L Jupiter Compact rail solves this completely: its manual ramp locking mechanism positively secures the TG-880 stretcher the moment loading completes, while the integrated backboard drawer ensures lifesaving auxiliary equipment travels safely within the same certified locking footprint. Every component is independently validated to the 10G crash standard — the same benchmark governing the TG-880 stretcher itself.
