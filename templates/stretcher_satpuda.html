Satpuda AST-919D Ambulance Stretcher

SKU: MED-STR-AST919D

The AST-919D is an AIS-125 certified ambulance stretcher specifically engineered for secure patient transport in demanding emergency mobile environments. Constructed from high-strength structural mild steel, it offers extreme reliability and stability during loading, unloading, and transit. Outfitted with collapsible side railings, an adjustable backrest, and an integrated I.V. rod system, this stretcher cot meets standard clinical criteria for modern ambulances across India.

  • ✓ Independent Folding Legs: Dual actuation levers for the front and rear legs ensure smooth loading and unloading transitions into the ambulance vehicle.
  • ✓ Collapsible Side Railings: Secure collapsible guardrail mechanism folds down cleanly to prevent interference with vehicle walls or medical gear during patient transfers.
  • ✓ Ambulance Floor Locking Base: Automatically secures in place when slid into the vehicle floor locking base, featuring a manual release lever for quick deployment.

Design, Comfort & Critical Features

Built to satisfy the automotive industry standard (AIS-125) requirements for ambulances in India, the Satpuda AST-919D brings high durability and operational security to mobile rescue teams. Its structural mild steel design provides a highly rigid and reliable platform capable of handling patients up to 170 kg safely.


Key Clinical & Mechanical Functions:

  • Backrest with 5 Fixing Positions: The backrest is easily adjustable up to 5 different lockable positions to support patient comfort. It can be flattened rapidly to perform CPR functions and is equipped with a sturdy backplate.
  • Provision for Leg Rest Elevation: Allows convenient leg rest elevation to position patients in a semi-fowler configuration, fully compliant with AIS-125 standards.
  • Castor Wheels with Brakes: Built with high-quality ball bearing castor wheels for smooth, effortless maneuverability in tight hallways. Active locking brakes on the rear wheels prevent accidental movement during patient loading.
