Wheelchair Stair Stretcher

SKU: MED-WCH-STAS301  |  Model: STAS 301

The STAS 301 is a CE certified, wheelchair-type stair stretcher engineered from premium anodized aluminium alloy. Compact, lightweight, and versatile, it is purpose-built for patient transport and evacuation through the most constrained environments — narrow hospital corridors, gangways, cloisters, fire-exit stairwells, and multi-turn access routes where conventional stretchers cannot operate. At just 9.2 kg net weight, it folds to an exceptionally slim 91 × 19 × 51 cm profile, enabling rapid storage and single-operator deployment across all clinical and emergency settings.

  • ✓ Dual-Configuration Design: Converts instantly between a compact folded transport mode (91 × 19 × 51 cm) and a full wheelchair-position layout (91 × 51 × 103 cm) for seamless staircase and corridor navigation.
  • ✓ 159 kg Structural Load Rating: Robust aluminium alloy chassis sustains patient loads up to 159 kg, built to handle the demands of real-world emergency evacuation operations.
  • ✓ Ultra-Lightweight at 9.2 kg: Anodized aluminium construction delivers exceptional strength-to-weight ratio — easy for single first-responder carry, deployment, and post-use storage in emergency lockers.

Engineered for Constrained Evacuation Environments

The STAS 301 was designed to solve the most critical gap in hospital and building emergency preparedness: safe, efficient patient transport in spaces that conventional stretchers cannot access. Its aluminium alloy frame offers the structural rigidity required for secure patient restraint on staircases and ramps while keeping the total unit weight below 10 kg — a figure that makes single-operator deployment genuinely practical during mass-casualty events or rapid ward evacuations.


The dual-configuration mechanism allows rapid switching between a fully folded storage profile and an operational wheelchair mode without the use of tools. The 49 cm seat height and 50 cm seat width conform to standard patient anthropometric dimensions, ensuring ergonomic comfort and positional safety across diverse patient profiles. The anodized aluminium surface treatment provides corrosion resistance for long-term hygiene compliance in hospital environments and extended field use in emergency response deployments.


Manufactured in India by Deluxe Scientific Surgico Pvt. Ltd. , a company with over five decades of medical equipment manufacturing heritage, the STAS 301 carries CE certification and is supplied to hospitals, nursing homes, ambulance services, and civil defence units at wholesale competitive prices. It is exported and supplied across India and to international procurement channels.
