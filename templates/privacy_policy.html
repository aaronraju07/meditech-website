Privacy Policy

Learn how Meditech Components collects, processes, and protects your information.

Last updated: June 24, 2026

At Meditech Components, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, store, and process your information when you use our website, which is dedicated to showcasing our medical technology product catalogs and collecting user product inquiries.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following personal data when you submit product inquiries through our online forms:

  • Full Name: Submitted to identify and communicate with you.
  • Institutional Email: Submitted to send formal inquiries, documentation, and product details.
  • Phone/Contact Number: Submitted to contact you for quick updates or procurement clarifications.
  • Message/Requirements: Any custom message or product specifications you provide via our inquiry forms.

2. How Data is Stored & Processed

We handle your collected information securely to fulfill your service requests:

  • Backend Database: All submitted inquiry information is saved securely in our database to maintain customer communication history.
  • Email Processing (Brevo): Your inquiry data is routed through Brevo (our third-party email service provider) to process, track, and respond to your inquiries.

3. Data Security

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures to protect your inquiry data against unauthorized access, loss, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Access to our database is restricted to authorized personnel who require it for customer service operations.

4. User Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal inquiry data we store, update or correct inaccurate details, or request the deletion of your inquiry history from our backend database. To exercise these rights, please contact us at our support email listed below.

5. Cookies and Log Files

This website utilizes standard technical cookies and server log files to maintain secure user sessions, enable stable website navigation, and apply rate limiting to prevent automated spam attacks. These cookies do not track your browsing activity across other websites.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request data updates or deletion, please contact us at:
