CS-200 Convertible Stretcher Chair

SKU: MED-STR-CS200

The CS-200 is an elite, highly adaptive convertible stretcher chair engineered from premium structural aluminium to be remarkably light and handy. Purpose-built to navigate difficult access places, it effortlessly transforms from a standard horizontal ambulance cot into an upright maneuverable rescue chair. With a maximum unassisted load rating of up to 300 kg and integrated crash-tested safety architecture, the CS-200 delivers ultimate performance for complex patient transport operations.

  • ✓ Dual-Staged Convertible Layout: Features effortless transformations between a full-length stretcher profile and an upright chair orientation to operate seamlessly in tight spaces.
  • ✓ Heavy-Duty 300 kg Rating: High-strength, specialized architectural frame built to comfortably lift and sustain up to 300 kg load configurations.
  • ✓ Comprehensive Treatment Features: Equipped standard with an adjustable shock absorber assisted backrest, assisted unfolding legs, a folding/removable IV drip stand, and an aluminum CPR tray for cardiac massage.

Clinical Articulation & Patient Security

Designed to satisfy modern emergency transport safety thresholds across multi-level egress fields, the KARTSANA CS-200 maximizes situational rescue handling. The articulating frame layout features an endless positioning system to comfortably align patient ergonomics alongside a comfortable 2-piece integrated mattress. While transferring patients longitudinally or laterally, security is continuously maintained through a professional 3-point safety belt restraint kit designed for the shoulders, abdomen, and legs.


Maneuvering through narrow staircases, elevators, or loading ramps is simplified via automated leg deployment steps and structural lengthwise collapsible safety armrests. On-the-ground mobility relies on four heavy-duty Ø150mm wheel assemblies, organized into 2 fixed front casters and 2 rear pivoting wheels outfitted with independent mechanical brake overrides to guarantee total tracking stability. The entire chassis complies with strict 10G dynamic crash testing safety benchmarks for seamless active vehicle placement.
