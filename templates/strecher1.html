TG-1000 IN Electric Incubator Stretcher

SKU: MED-STR-TG1000IN

The TG-1000 IN is a premium-tier, lightweight aluminum powered stretcher optimized specifically for critical neonatal and paediatric patient transport services. Engineered to interface seamlessly with heavy medical incubators, this heavy-duty system features intelligent electric lift mechanics capable of an unassisted lifting capacity of up to 300 kg. Its rugged, structural design delivers complete crash safety compliance, making it the premier option for clinical emergency mobile deployment.

  • ✓ Incubator Compatibility: Purpose-built frame layout perfectly compatible with specialized clinical incubators for paediatric transport operations.
  • ✓ Unassisted 300 kg Capacity: High-torque electric lift mechanics effortlessly manage a load capacity of up to 300 kg completely unassisted.
  • ✓ Fail-Safe Hydraulic Security: Outfitted with an active safety mechanism to completely prevent accidental collapse, paired with a dedicated hydraulic system override button to guarantee unlocking controls in the event of an electrical fault.

Clinical Performance & Maneuverability

Designed to satisfy the highest safety thresholds in professional mobile medicine, the TG-1000 IN Stretcher brings superior versatility to ambulance and emergency transport fleets. Built using high-grade, resilient structural aluminium, it ensures a highly rigid platform while keeping total vehicle payload weight minimal. The stretcher features an fully adjustable operational working height to facilitate seamless patient transfers across varying clinical platform configurations.


Maneuverability is controlled through four oversized $\varnothing\text{ 160 mm}$ rotating wheel assemblies. This heavy-duty caster orientation incorporates 2 localized locking wheels alongside 2 specialized wheels outfitted with dedicated mechanical brake units, ensuring total control on inclines or during tight ambulance tracking steps. Note: The optional modular upper tray panel (Reference C-004) can be paired for non-incubator specialized operations but is not included standard with base reference model TG-1000 IN.

Why a Certified Ambulance Stretcher Lock System Matters

In emergency medical transport, a certified stretcher locking system is not optional — it is a clinical safety mandate. Stretcher movement during ambulance transit can cause secondary patient injuries and compromise IV lines or airway management equipment. The R-1002A BRAVO lateral telescopic lock eliminates all in-vehicle stretcher displacement, providing EMS crews a hands-free, 10G crash-certified anchor point that keeps the TG-1000 stretcher firmly fixed to the ambulance floor rail from the moment the loading sequence completes until deliberate release is triggered.
