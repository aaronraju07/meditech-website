TG-241 Ambulance Stretcher

SKU: MED-STR-TG241

The TG-241 is KARTSANA's high-performance aluminum ambulance stretcher engineered for demanding emergency medical transport. It delivers a 250 kg load capacity, a reclining backrest with leg-rest and shock absorber, and laterally and longitudinally collapsible guardrails that make patient transfers fast and safe. Four lockable wheels — including twin wheels on the leading edge for enhanced directional stability — combine with Trendelenburg and reverse Trendelenburg positioning to meet the full spectrum of clinical needs while minimising effort for EMS crews. Certified to the 10G dynamic crash standard, the TG-241 is confirmed compatible with the 241 Series R-419 Jupiter Compact rail.

  • ✓ 250 kg Load Capacity: Robust aluminum chassis sustains a full 250 kg unassisted structural load, supporting a wide range of patient body types.
  • ✓ Collapsible Guardrails — Lateral & Longitudinal: Guardrails collapse in both axes for streamlined patient transfer, significantly reducing crew effort and ambulance bay clearance demands.
  • ✓ Trendelenburg & Reverse Trendelenburg: Clinically important positioning modes are built in, helping healthcare workers manage patient condition during transit with minimal physical effort.

Clinical Engineering & Rail Compatibility

The TG-241 is KARTSANA's proven multi-purpose ambulance stretcher, built on a lightweight yet rigid aluminum chassis purpose-designed for demanding pre-hospital environments. Six core feature groups define the TG-241: a 250 kg structural load capacity, a fully reclining backrest with integrated leg-rest and shock absorber for smooth patient handling, and laterally and longitudinally collapsible guardrails that fold away completely to streamline narrow-corridor and doorway transfer. The chassis rolls on four independently lockable wheels — with a twin-wheel configuration on the leading edge — delivering maximum traction and directional stability on any ambulance ramp or hospital floor surface.


For clinical positioning, the TG-241 provides both Trendelenburg (feet-up) and reverse Trendelenburg (head-up) orientations, reducing physical strain on healthcare workers during treatment while in transit. The stretcher's "double the wheels, double the ease of loading" principle means that both wheels can be locked or unlocked simultaneously on the TG model for safe, controlled ambulance loading. The TG-241 is confirmed compatible with the 241 Series R-419 Jupiter Compact floor rail, delivering a unified 10G-certified stretcher-to-ambulance-floor chain. All KARTSANA products carry compliance certification and are independently validated to the 10G dynamic crash safety threshold.

R-419 / R-419L

Range of Rails for 241 COMPACT Series Ambulance Stretchers — Confirmed Compatible with TG-241

The R-419 / R-419L is a factory-certified ambulance floor rail system from the 241 COMPACT SERIES, confirmed compatible with the TG-241 stretcher. Built from aluminium, it provides a rigid, secure track for emergency stretcher transit. The system features a manual locking/unlocking entry ramp for controlled stretcher docking and an integrated backboard or scoop stretcher drawer for dedicated emergency equipment storage — all independently certified to the 10G dynamic crash standard, matching the crash safety level of the TG-241 itself.

  • ✓ Factory-Confirmed TG-241 Compatibility: The R-419 is verified by the manufacturer for direct use with the TG-241 in 241 Series-configured ambulances, ensuring first-time fitment without adaptation.
  • ✓ Backboard & Scoop Stretcher Storage: Built-in drawer provides secure in-ambulance storage for a full-size backboard or scoop stretcher, keeping critical rescue equipment instantly accessible without occupying additional cabin space.
  • ✓ Manual Ramp Lock / Unlock: Precise manual locking ramp mechanism positively engages and releases the stretcher from the rail without tools, enabling fast, controlled loading and unloading.
  • ✓ 10G Dynamic Crash Certified: Every structural element of the R-419 rail system is independently validated under the 10G crash standard — the same benchmark as the TG-241 — ensuring the complete stretcher-rail assembly meets a unified safety certification.

Compatibility Note: The R-419 rail system is factory-confirmed compatible with the TG-241 in the 241 Series. The R-419L suits extended-wheelbase ambulances and is compatible with all Jupiter Trolley Series models except the TG-880GC4. Contact our team to confirm the optimal rail variant for your fleet.

Why the TG-241 + R-419 Rail Is the Complete Ambulance Transport Solution

Demanding emergency transport requires a stretcher and rail system that are certified to the same crash standard. The TG-241 provides the high-capacity, clinically versatile platform; the R-419 / R-419L provides the certified floor anchorage. Together, they form a dual 10G-certified chain from stretcher deck to ambulance floor. The Trendelenburg positioning capability means crews can treat patients during transit, while the integrated backboard drawer ensures resuscitation-critical equipment travels in the same secured envelope — eliminating unsecured loose items in the patient compartment.
